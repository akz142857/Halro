# Complete WorkUnit Outcome history

`GET /halro/v1/work-units/{workUnitID}` retains the `work_unit`, `runs` and `outcomes` response fields. Outcomes include every revision of the WorkUnit's frozen definition versions, in native Governance sequence order. They are not replaced by current heads or silently truncated to a page.

The handler obtains the WorkUnit, declared definitions, Runs and inflight observation from the current accounting owner at one Ledger cut. It then reads Outcome facts at a separate current Governance cut. The two journals do not form an atomic snapshot. Every returned Outcome's provisional flag uses the earlier accounting observation: open WorkUnit or accepted-request/pending-attempt activity means provisional; closing alone does not prove maturity.

The declarations come from accounting, not client query parameters. A WorkUnit freezes at most eight definitions, with at most twenty revisions each: the complete history has at most 160 rows. `ReadWorkUnitOutcomes` holds the Manager operation through one State read lock or one disk View, checks live native ownership and aligned source positions, and reads the existing WorkUnit index. It does not invoke a full snapshot or fall back to State when a disk owner fails. The State path was already indexed by WorkUnit; this change does not claim to remove a prior whole-history scan from that path.

The disk path examines at most 160 physical index rows, including excluded foreign-project rows, and checks Record JSON size before decode (192 KiB per row). The accumulated source ceiling is `160 × 192 KiB + 8 × 66 B` (30 MiB plus 528 B, including current-head JSON). Returned Outcome JSON is at most 64 KiB per row and 10 MiB in total, derived from native Governance payload and domain limits. State guards field sizes before encoding a finite source-meter copy. These bounds do not prove allocation peaks, mmap/RSS behavior, shared RAM ownership or production capacity.

Rows must match the WorkUnit, frozen definition ID/version and native position. The reader restores native sequence order, requires increasing sequences and contiguous revisions/Supersedes chains, and compares each declaration's final returned Outcome ID with its current head. This detects individual history/index gaps, including a missing tail or entire definition history when the head witness remains. It does not authenticate persisted key sets: coordinated removal of both history and its head can appear absent. D-03 content/key-set authentication and trusted restart remain unfinished; a fresh exclusive disk candidate still requires full authenticated native-log reconstruction and a trusted role-permitted head.

Invalid declarations, row overflow, byte overflow, chain/scope/index/source errors, cancellation and unavailable/closed owners discard the entire result. The handler publishes HTTP 503 without partial `work_unit`, `runs` or `outcomes`. A valid definition with no reported Outcome returns an empty history. Mutex and native IO waits are not forcibly interruptible.

Default Runtime/Replica residency, export snapshots, production owner drain/generation publication, trusted recovery, complete shared budgets and Linux/production acceptance remain pending. Test-only disk-owner selection after a real producer drain stays draining and does not exercise production publication.
