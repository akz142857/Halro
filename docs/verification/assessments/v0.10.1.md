## Defect and review disposition

Every change in the range reached `main` through a pull request whose gate and
CI were green. No independent second-review claim is made. Found and fixed
inside the range rather than carried:

- #466: Halro forwarded `temperature`/`top_p`/`top_k` to Claude models that
  reject them, so the upstream answered 400 after the budget was reserved.
- #467: the built-in catalogue offered MiniMax's retired M2 line (404 on every
  face while `/v1/models` still lists it). While that PR was open,
  `pip-audit` began reporting three new `filelock` advisories in the Python
  SDK compatibility lock; bumped in the same PR.
- #469: `stream_max_duration` cut long reasoning streams at 10m; and the
  Debian and AWS KMS systemd units killed Halro after 150s, short of the 10m
  `shutdown_timeout` v0.10.0 introduced — a v0.10.0 regression #451 missed.

The repository had no open issues labelled `bug` when queried on 2026-10-24.
Known limitations recorded in earlier assessments remain.

## Invariants and upgrade impact

1. **Single writer / data directory:** untouched.
2. **Ledger authority:** untouched; Ledger and usage agree at every smoke stage.
3. **Fail closed:** strengthened — a sampling member an unknown Claude model
   might reject is refused before reservation, and MiniMax identifiers with no
   measured entry stay undeployable.
4. **Secret handling:** untouched; secret-canary scan passed.
5. **Replay determinism:** untouched.
6. **Bounded retries/fallback:** the stream bound is longer for new
   installs (30m) and remains explicit; existing configurations keep theirs.
   A sampling refusal is decided before any attempt, so it consumes none.
7. **Capability filtering:** per-target sampling rules for the three Anthropic
   Messages profiles; MiniMax's metered profiles lose the M2 entries.
8. **Compatibility manifests:** the three Anthropic profiles' manifests declare
   the sampling rule; goldens regenerated (#466).
9. **Configuration:** one new default, `gateway.stream_max_duration` 30m. The
   published snapshot differs from v0.10.0's in exactly that key (and its
   comment). The systemd units' `TimeoutStopSec` is 650s.

Re-initialisation required from v0.10.0: **no**. Rollback fence: **none** —
schema unchanged; v0.10.0 was observed starting and serving on a directory the
candidate wrote, and restoring the pre-upgrade backup also works.
