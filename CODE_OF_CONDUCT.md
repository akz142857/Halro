# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Halro contributors and maintainers commit to a respectful, inclusive, and
professional community. Participation must be free from harassment regardless
of experience, identity, background, nationality, appearance, disability,
religion, or technical preference.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Assume good intent while accepting clear correction.
- Give actionable, technically grounded review feedback.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's credentials or personal data.
- Disclose conflicts of interest that could affect project decisions.
- Accept maintainer decisions on scope, safety, and release readiness.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, stalking, or sexualized conduct;
- insults, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, or sustained bad-faith debate;
- publishing private information without permission;
- attempting to obtain or misuse project, maintainer, or user credentials;
- retaliating against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository activity, community spaces, and public events
where someone represents Halro. Maintainers may edit or remove content,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when needed
to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's GitHub private
vulnerability reporting channel. Select an appropriate category and state that
the report concerns community conduct; do not include unrelated secrets. If
private reporting is unavailable, open a public issue containing only a request
for private maintainer contact, without sensitive details.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical. A
maintainer involved in a report must recuse themselves from handling it.
